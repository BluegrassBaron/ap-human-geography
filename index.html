Each unit is a single self-contained page — no build step, no dependencies, no tracking. Open it on a phone or a Chromebook, or print it; the layout handles all three.

Pages open light, on paper-colored stock — there is a dark switch in the header if you prefer it. Printing gives you a clean black-on-white handout with no setting to change: one topic per sheet, every self-check answer opened out.

About these notes

Written and maintained by Jordan Evans, who teaches AP Human Geography at Bourbon County High School in Paris, Kentucky. Built for his students, and free for anyone else who finds them useful.
